# Privacy

Cookie Eater processes consent banners locally on your Mac. When Apple Intelligence is available and enabled, banner text, a compact consent-dialog DOM snapshot, and candidate controls go to Apple’s on-device Foundation Models framework. Cookie Eater does not send them to a server.

The extension stores per-site pause settings, the AI preference, and up to 100 recent outcomes in Safari’s local extension storage. Outcomes contain the site and frame hostnames, time, provider, decision source, and result. They do not contain full URLs, page content, banner text, or cookie values. Private-window outcomes are not stored.

You can clear recent activity from the Safari toolbar popup. No Cookie Eater account, analytics, telemetry, cloud inference, or remote rule downloads are used.
