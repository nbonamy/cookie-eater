# Privacy declarations

Proposed App Store answer: **Data Not Collected**. Cookie Eater does not transmit browsing content, consent-dialog snapshots, cookie values, outcomes, identifiers, analytics, or telemetry to its developer or a third party. Foundation Models inference runs on the user's Mac.

Local extension storage holds per-site pause settings, the AI preference, and at most 100 recent outcomes containing hostnames, timestamps, provider, source, result, and evidence. It excludes full URLs, page content, banner text, and cookie values. Private-window outcomes are excluded. The popup can clear the history.

Tracking: **No**. No advertising, analytics, third-party tracking SDKs, account system, or external inference endpoint.

The public privacy policy and support destinations are in metadata.en-US.json. They are also available from the containing app.

Complete the age-rating, content-rights, accessibility, and export-compliance questionnaires against the final build in App Store Connect. Do not claim accessibility features that have not been verified. No custom encryption implementation is included.
