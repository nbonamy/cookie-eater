Fewer banners. More browsing.

Cookie Eater handles cookie consent automatically in Safari on Mac. It rejects optional cookies when possible, works through supported settings and legitimate-interest choices, and accepts when no free refusal option is available. Ambiguous choices stay untouched.

LOCAL BY DESIGN
Familiar banners use bundled rules. When those cannot continue, Apple's on-device Foundation Models interpret the consent dialog in its language. Your browsing content and inference stay on your Mac. No account, telemetry, or cloud inference.

YOU STAY IN CONTROL
Pause Cookie Eater on any website, retry a banner, turn Apple Intelligence on or off, and review or clear recent activity from the Safari toolbar. Private-window activity is not saved.

CLEAR OUTCOMES
Cookie Eater distinguishes requested rejection, verified rejection, acceptance to continue, and banners that need your attention. A banner disappearing alone never counts as verified rejection.

GET STARTED
Open Cookie Eater, enable the extension in Safari Settings, and allow access to the websites you choose. It then runs automatically. You can quit the companion app.

Requires macOS 14 or later. On-device AI requires macOS 26 or later, a supported Mac, and an available Apple Intelligence model. Bundled rules remain available without AI. Some websites and languages may require a manual choice.
